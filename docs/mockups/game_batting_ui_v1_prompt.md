Replace navy cap with simple matching unbranded NAVY BATTING HELMET for the game. Both hands correctly gripping ONE wooden bat held back above right shoulder, knees slightly flexed, feet planted. Do not face viewer, no T pose, no large portrait insert.
REFERENCE 2 supplies visual style only: quiet navy ink contours, warm ivory, dusty blue, matte flat shading and restrained paper texture. DO NOT preserve training UI header/tabs/room split, five rows or training scene. This must be real gameplay composition.
CAMERA: behind the right-handed batter looking directly toward the pitcher mound and center field. Batter occupies left foreground, whole figure visible in left third between x18–37%, feet near y86%, helmet y30%. Leave a clear unobstructed center lane from home plate to mound. Pitcher small in middle distance centered x53%, y43%, opponent pale uniform with muted terracotta accents and unbranded cap; one right-arm throw mid-followthrough. One small bright white baseball in flight centered x53%, y53%, readable against field, very short restrained trailing stroke. Pitcher's mound must be beyond home plate. No catcher standing in front of pitcher or blocking strike zone. Camera behind catcher position; catcher not visible. Small distant infielders at appropriate field positions, no huge crowd.
ENVIRONMENT: modest Korean school competition field, sandy dirt infield and muted sage outfield, simple chain-link perimeter, low stands with tiny sparse abstract spectators, simple school buildings and hills, pale dusty-blue sunny spring sky. Same handmade restrained inked flat-color aesthetic as reference, NOT lush shiny anime, not photorealistic, not 3D, no real signs/brands. Foul lines originate homeplate foreground and diverge naturally into field. Home plate low center. Front-facing white thin STRIKE ZONE OVERLAY centered x53%, y65%, clear rectangular upright 3×3 grid, width ~13% screen, height ~24%. Small muted-orange target ring in middle cell, no enormous glowing reticle. Home plate must lie beneath zone. Ball lane aligned mound→zone→plate.
