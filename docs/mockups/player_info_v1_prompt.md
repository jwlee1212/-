Create ONE polished actual PLAYER INFORMATION screen UI mockup for a Korean casual baseball career mobile game FIRST PICK. Landscape 2.16:1, equivalent to 844×390 logical pixels. No device frame, no annotation.
REFERENCE: attached T-pose supplies EXACT main character identity and costume and drawing style only. REPLACE T-POSE with a relaxed full-body standing pose, arms lowered, one hand resting on hip, other naturally at side, no bat. Same teenage boy's face, black hair, navy cap, warm ivory uniform and pants, navy undershirt and socks, ivory sneakers; same body/head proportions. Use reference's navy hand-inked contours, matte flat cel shading with minimal shadows, subdued warm colors and sparing paper texture. No glossy anime, photorealism or 3D.
Match previously established cream/navy/burnt-orange understated handmade sports-game interface. Quiet player bedroom environment: left side pale dusty blue wall, small simple wooden locker and baseball glove on a shelf as faint scenic hints, no visual clutter. Most background continuous warm ivory.

Top slim ivory bar ~11% height: left back arrow "골목으로", center bold "선수 정보", right small muted "1학년 · 봄". Thin navy bottom rule.
Below header a single horizontal tab strip ~11% height, tabs exact "능력치", "장비", "성장 기록", "수상·기념". "능력치" selected with navy text and burnt-orange underline; other tabs muted navy. No extra tabs.
Main content split LEFT 35% character area / RIGHT 59% information with a fine minimal divider, NOT a floating dashboard grid. Full body boy occupies left, cap and feet fully visible, standing in front of quiet illustrated room, slightly smiling. At left bottom beneath figure two lines "내 선수" and "서림고 · 1학년", use "내 선수" as intentional placeholder since user chooses name.

Each row has small monoline icon, exact label, long horizontal progress bar with solid muted navy fill on warm pale gray track, then a bold grade and clear value aligned at far right. Exact rows:
"컨택" grade "D" value "42"
"파워" grade "D" value "38"

Bottom of right panel one understated small inline note "최근 성장" followed by three compact text items "컨택 +2", "선구안 +1", "주력 +1" with burnt-orange plus numbers. Below that a thin separator and footer line "성장 단계  성장기" in muted navy with simple sprout line icon, no invented XP or level.
No giant CTA: this is an information screen. No currencies, premium gems, overall rating, potential number, radar chart, character customization, position selector, skill tree, energy, unnecessary buttons or experience bars. No recommended names or game start. No extra freeform slogans. Keep UI flat and thoughtful, small corner radii, simple rules and precise typography, no glossy bevels, minimal shadows. All labels accurate Korean and inside 5% safe margins. No real logos or watermark.
