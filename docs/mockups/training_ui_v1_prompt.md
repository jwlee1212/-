Use case: ui-mockup
Create ONE actual TRAINING MENU UI mockup for Korean baseball career mobile game FIRST PICK. Landscape aspect ratio 2.16:1 (844×390 logical mobile screen). The supplied player information mockup is the EXACT visual-system and main-character reference. Keep same cream paper, navy handmade ink contours, muted dusty blue and ochre, flat matte cel-shaded illustrations, sparse paper texture, thin divider rules, generous legible Korean type. Burnt orange reserved for selected-tab underline and primary action. No glossy anime/3D. No device frame or watermark.
Recompose from player information into SCHOOL TRAINING GROUND.
TOP header: back arrow and "골목으로" left, centered bold "훈련", right "1학년 · 봄".
Under header a single three-tab bar "구간 훈련", "개인 레슨", "관리". Select "구간 훈련" with navy text and burnt-orange underline. All headers same height and style as reference.
LEFT 34% scene: same recognizable canonical boy black tousled hair/navy cap/off-white uniform/navy undershirt and socks/ivory sneakers in relaxed batting practice ready stance holding ONE wooden bat naturally with both hands; full figure visible including feet, not a dramatic swing. Background spare side view of school's sand practice ground, chain link fence and simple equipment basket, inked and flat colors. Same boy face/body proportions, not a redesigned adult. Small navy caption at bottom of scene "서림고 운동장".
RIGHT 61% UI:
Top compact line "봄 리그 · 구간 시작" then large heading "이번 구간의 훈련 방향". Small secondary "하나를 고르면 이번 구간에 적용돼요".
FIVE neatly aligned compact selectable horizontal rows with thin gray-blue dividers, original small monoline icons at left, bold label, short description and radio selection indicator at far right:
1 "컨택" | "배트에 맞히는 능력" — SELECTED, navy thin outline around this row with subtle pale blue fill and filled navy radio. Small bat-and-ball icon.
2 "파워" | "타구에 힘을 싣는 능력" — unselected radio. Simple strength icon.
3 "선구안" | "공을 보고 골라내는 능력" — unselected radio. Eye icon.
4 "주루" | "달리기와 베이스 플레이" — unselected radio. Running shoe icon.
5 "휴식" | "몸 상태 관리" — unselected radio. Simple restful crescent icon.
No week calendar, no schedule slots or success stamps. Rows large enough to tap and all five visible at once.
Below rows a small quiet inline summary "선택한 방향  |  컨택" then one wide burnt-orange button with white bold text "훈련 방향 확정 →". A tiny footer caption under or beside button "이번 구간에 적용". Button fully within safe bottom margin.
Do not show an immediate exercise mini-game, individual-lesson content, management content or training results in this screen. These are unselected tabs. Do NOT invent costs, guaranteed numeric gains, training duration, random success rates, XP, gems, stamina spending, paid speedup, level or upgrade points. No defense training, no fielding stats, no auto-match/simulation buttons. ONE training direction only for whole segment, not repeated weekly choices. Exact Korean labels accurate. Main art and UI should feel same family as reference but original training composition, no bedroom backdrop, no player stat bars, no extra slogan. Clean readable visual hierarchy in a restrained handmade sports game.
